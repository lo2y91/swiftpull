SwiftPull

Downloads and file transfer between your devices.

SwiftPull is a fast download manager for Windows and Android. It also sends links and files between your phone, tablet and computer over your own Wi-Fi, encrypted, with no account and no cloud.

Download for Windows Windows 10 and 11 Download for Android Phones and tablets, Android 10+

Free. Have a Chromebook? Get the Chromebook file

What it does

One app on each device, working together.

DOWNLOADS

Fast, and it picks up where it stopped

Each file comes over several connections at once. Pause, resume after a dropped connection, line downloads up in queues, or schedule them for the night.

VIDEO AND MUSIC

Choose the quality before it starts

See the size of each quality first, keep just the audio as MP3, add subtitles, or take only a part of a long video. On Android, play it in the built-in player or shrink it for sharing.

BETWEEN DEVICES

Phone to computer, and back

Share a link on your phone and it starts on the computer. Send a finished video from the computer to the tablet from the right-click menu, including Windows' own Send to menu.

TABLETS

Made for the big screen too

A two-pane layout on tablets and Chromebooks, a floating video window while you use other apps, keyboard shortcuts, and drag a link in from the browser.

PRIVATE FOLDER

Files only you can open

Move a download into the private folder on Android: it leaves the gallery and other apps, and opens only with your fingerprint or screen lock.

ARABIC AND ENGLISH

In your language, and it updates itself

A full Arabic interface with right-to-left layout, and English. Both apps check for new versions and install them when you say so.

Your files stay yours

  • No account, no cloud Files go straight from one of your devices to the other over your own Wi-Fi. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  • Encrypted on the way Links and files travel encrypted (AES-256). Devices pair once with a six-digit code that is never sent over the network.
  • Signed updates The Windows app only accepts updates signed by the publisher, and Android only installs an update signed with the same key.

Install it

Both are free. Put it on the computer and on the phone to use the link between them.

Windows

  1. Download SwiftPull-Setup.exe with the button above and open it.
  2. Windows may warn about an app it doesn't know yet: choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. It installs for your user only; no administrator password is needed.

Android

  1. Download the APK with the button above, on the phone itself.
  2. Open it. Android asks once to allow installing from your browser: allow it.
  3. To link with the computer: Settings → Computer on the phone, and Options → Phone on the computer.
